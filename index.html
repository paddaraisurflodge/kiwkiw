Our Story

Paddarai Surf Lodge is a family-owned and locally managed surf retreat, built in April 2026 and completed in October 2026. Located right on the beachfront in Malakopa, South Pagai Island, the lodge sits only a few meters from the famous Roxy's surf spot, giving surfers easy access to many of the best waves in the Mentawai Islands.

Designed with a simple tropical cottage concept, Paddarai Surf Lodge consists of three buildings: a cozy beachfront bar and two comfortable accommodation cottages. Our location was carefully chosen in the heart of several world-class surf spots, making every surf trip easy, convenient, and unforgettable.

All Paddarai crew members are local people who know the island, the waves, and the culture very well. We are dedicated to creating a warm and friendly atmosphere where every guest can feel at home, enjoy great surfing, and experience the true spirit of Mentawai hospitality.

Our Boat
On the Water

Our Boat

At Paddarai Surf Lodge, we use a local long boat powered by a 40HP engine to support our guests’ activities throughout their stay. The boat is used for surf trips, exploring many surf breaks, as well as transfers to and from the Mentawai Fast ferry pier in Sikakap. With experienced local crew members, getting to the surf spots and traveling between the lodge and the harbor is easy. For us, the boat is more than just transportation — it’s part of the experience of enjoying the surfing lifestyle and island journey in Mentawai.

Surf Breaks

Paddarai Surf Lodge is located right in the middle between the surf breaks of North and South Pagai, making surf trips to various waves easier and more convenient.

Roxy's

Roxies is a fun and playful right-hand reef break located not far from Paddarai Surf Lodge. The wave offers long open walls perfect for carving, top turns, and smooth performance surfing. On smaller swells, Roxies becomes an easy and playful wave for all levels, while bigger swells bring beautiful barrel sections with a clean, flowing rhythm. Many surfers describe it as a “Macas in reverse” thanks to its perfectly lined-up shape and enjoyable pace.

South Pagai
0,47 miles

Rag's Left

Rag’s Left is a powerful left-hand reef break located around 10-15 minutes from Paddarai Surf Lodge in the southern Mentawai area. Known for its long hollow barrels and fast walls, this wave offers an exciting ride for experienced surfers. On the right swell, Rag’s Left can produce long tubing sections followed by a more open wall perfect for powerful turns down the line. Breaking over a shallow coral reef, the wave becomes heavier and more challenging as the swell increases.

South Pagai
4,89 miles

Rag's Right

Rag’s Right is a fast and powerful right-hand reef break located around 15-20 minutes from Paddarai Surf Lodge. Known for its hollow barrels and clean racing sections, this wave offers some of the best tubing conditions in South Mentawai when the swell and tide line up. Breaking over a shallow coral reef, Rag’s Right is best suited for advanced surfers who are comfortable with fast, heavy waves and committed tube rides.

South Pagai
5,80 miles

Thunders

Thunders is a powerful and consistent left-hand reef break located around 20-25 minutes from Paddarai Surf Lodge. Known for its heavy walls, bowl sections, and dependable swell, Thunders works well across different tides and conditions, making it one of the most reliable waves nearby. On smaller to medium swells, the wave offers fun and clean walls, while bigger swells bring faster and more challenging sections for experienced surfers. With multiple take-off spots and long workable rides.

South Pagai
7,57 miles

Thunder Box

Thunder Box is a heavy and hollow right-hand reef break located around 25-30 minutes from Paddarai Surf Lodge. Although the wave is relatively short, it is famous for its thick and powerful barrels that can run from take-off to finish. Thunder Box works best with the right swell direction and tide, and when the conditions line up, it delivers some of the most exciting tube rides in the area. Best suited for experienced surfers, this wave offers a fast, intense, and unforgettable session.

South Pagai
9,12 miles

Greenbush

Greenbush is a world-class left-hand reef break located around 30-40 minutes from Paddarai Surf Lodge in the southern Mentawai area. Famous for its thick, deep, and perfectly round barrels, Greenbush offers one of the most unforgettable tube rides in the Mentawais when the conditions line up. The wave breaks over a shallow coral reef and works best on higher tides with solid south swells. Fast, heavy, and extremely hollow, Greenbush is recommended only for advanced surfers who are comfortable in powerful barreling waves.

North Pagai
12,31 miles

Macaronis

Macaronis is one of the most famous and enjoyable waves in the Mentawai Islands, located around 40–60 minutes from Paddarai Surf Lodge. This world-class left-hand reef break is known for its perfect shape, long playful walls, and incredibly consistent barrels. Macaronis works well on almost all tides and offers everything from smooth carving sections to fast tubing waves, making it a favorite for surfers from around the world. On smaller days it’s fun for intermediate surfers, while bigger swells turn the wave into a fast and powerful ride better suited for experienced surfers.

North Pagai
18,10 miles

How to Get Here

Reaching Paddarai Surf Lodge is part of the adventure. Follow these steps to get to our little corner of paradise in the Mentawai Islands.

Step 01
By Plane

Fly to Minangkabau International Airport

Your journey begins with a flight to Minangkabau International Airport (PDG) in Padang, West Sumatra. Direct flights are available from Jakarta, Kuala Lumpur, and other major hubs.

Varies by origin
Step 02
By Ferry

Ferry from Padang to Sikakap

After arriving in Padang, you’ll need to stay overnight before continuing your journey. The next day, from Muaro Harbour in Padang, take the Mentawai Fast ferry to East Sikakap Pier in Sikakap (North Pagai Island). Please note that the Mentawai Fast schedule for the Padang ↔ Sikakap route is only available once a week, every Friday. Make sure you already have your Mentawai Surf Tax (mandatory surf tax for surfers) before departure.

3–5 hours
Step 03
By Boat

Sikakap to Lodge

After arriving at East Sikakap Pier, the Paddarai Surf Lodge team will be waiting to pick you up directly at the pier, so you won’t have to wait long. The journey to the lodge will then continue by Paddarai Surf Lodge’s longboat.

~30-40 Minutes
